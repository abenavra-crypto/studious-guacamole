About

Literary management and development—powered by a passion for great stories and the expertise to get them made.

Ari Ben-Avram

Ari Ben-Avram is a Literary Manager and Development Executive at Chatrone, an international management and production company whose credits include the Golden Globe-nominated The Book of Life (co-produced with Guillermo del Toro) and Aztec Batman: Clash of Empires (2025) for Warner Bros. Animation.

He holds an MFA from the John Wells Division of Writing for Screen & Television at USC.

Previously, he has worked with Jay Roach (Recount, Game Change, The Roses, Bombshell) and Doug Belgrad at 2.0 Entertainment (Bad Boys for Life, Peter Rabbit 2: The Runaway).

My Approach

I read everything with two lenses: the creative lens and the market lens. The first asks, “Is this a good story, well told?” The second asks, “Can this get made, and how?”

Too many consultants focus on one and ignore the other. I believe both perspectives are essential. The best material in the world won’t get produced if it’s pitched to the wrong buyers. And the most marketable concept won’t succeed if the execution isn’t strong.

My job is to help you excel on both fronts.
