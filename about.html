About Me

Experience development executive with a passion for great stories and the expertise to get them made.

My Story

I've spent my career at the intersection of creative storytelling and commercial strategy. As an experience development executive, I've read thousands of scripts, manuscripts, and pitches—and I've learned to quickly identify what makes material resonate with audiences and what makes it attractive to buyers.

My background spans film, television, publishing, and emerging media. I've worked with first-time writers and seasoned professionals alike, helping them refine their material and navigate the path from draft to deal.

I believe every writer deserves honest, insightful, and constructive feedback—the kind that makes the work better and makes the path forward clearer. That's what I deliver.

My Approach

I read everything with two lenses: the creative lens and the market lens. The first asks, "Is this a good story, well told?" The second asks, "Can this get made, and how?"

Too many consultants focus on one and ignore the other. I believe both perspectives are essential. The best material in the world won't get produced if it's pitched to the wrong buyers. And the most marketable concept won't succeed if the execution isn't strong.

My job is to help you excel on both fronts.
