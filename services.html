Services

Comprehensive feedback that addresses both the art and the business of storytelling.

Creative Quality Analysis

Every great project starts with strong material. I provide a thorough creative evaluation that examines the fundamental building blocks of your work:

Structure & Plot — Is the architecture of your story sound? Does it build tension, maintain momentum, and deliver a satisfying resolution?

Character Development — Are your characters dimensional, consistent, and compelling? Do they drive the story rather than serve it?

Theme & Meaning — Does the work say something? Is the thematic throughline clear without being heavy-handed?

Dialogue — Does each character have a distinct voice? Is the dialogue natural, purposeful, and revealing of character?

Pacing & Tone — Does the work maintain the right rhythm? Is the tone consistent and appropriate for the genre and audience?

What You Receive

A detailed written report (8–15 pages depending on material length) that includes:

  • Executive summary with overall assessment
  • Scene-by-scene or chapter-by-chapter notes
  • Character analysis for each principal role
  • Specific, actionable revision suggestions
  • Comparative titles and genre positioning

Marketability Report Includes

  • Target audience identification
  • Competitive landscape analysis
  • Comparable projects and their performance
  • Recommended buyers, studios, or publishers
  • Packaging and talent attachment suggestions
  • Pitch strategy and timing recommendations

Marketability Assessment

A brilliant script sitting in a drawer helps no one. My marketability analysis bridges the gap between creative work and commercial reality.

I evaluate your material through the lens of current market demand—what buyers are looking for, which genres are trending, and where the opportunities lie.

You'll understand not just if your project is marketable, but how to position it, who to take it to, and what adjustments could broaden its appeal without compromising its integrity.
